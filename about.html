About This Portal

PM SHRI Schools of Uttar Pradesh

This portal brings together all 160 PM SHRI (PM Schools for Rising India) Rajkiya Inter Colleges selected across Uttar Pradesh under a centrally sponsored scheme by the Ministry of Education, Government of India.

PM SHRI schools are developed as exemplar institutions that showcase implementation of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, offering a modern, equitable, and joyful learning experience.

Each school on this portal maintains its own profile with details such as location, staff, facilities, and contact information, managed through a dedicated school login.

Our Vision

To make every PM SHRI school in Uttar Pradesh a model of excellence, nurturing well-rounded, confident, and responsible students equipped to thrive in a rapidly changing world.

🚀

Our Mission

To provide a single, transparent directory of PM SHRI schools across the state, and to support each school in delivering equitable, high-quality, NEP-aligned education.

PM SHRI Yojana

What Is PM SHRI?

Understanding the scheme behind these schools.

🏫

Model Schools

PM SHRI schools are developed as exemplar institutions, upgrading existing schools across India rather than creating new ones.

📘

NEP 2020 Aligned

Implements competency-based, experiential learning with an emphasis on 21st-century skills and holistic report cards.

🌍

Sustainable Campuses

Focus on green practices, solar power, waste management, and eco-friendly infrastructure across school campuses.
